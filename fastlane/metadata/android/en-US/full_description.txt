A free and open-source scientific calculator with natural math input, exact results and step-by-step solutions. Expressions are shown the way you would write them on paper: fractions, powers, roots, integrals and matrices are displayed in proper two-dimensional notation while you type. It solves equations, differentiates and integrates, works with matrices, units and complex numbers, and draws 2D and 3D graphs.

<b>Features</b>

<ul>
<li><b>Natural display:</b> Fractions, exponents, roots, integrals and matrices rendered as proper math notation while you type, with tap-to-place cursor, selection, copy and paste</li>
<li><b>Live results:</b> The result is worked out while you type; exact where possible (fractions, roots, π) and switchable to decimal, scientific, mixed number, polar or HEX/OCT/BIN form</li>
<li><b>Step-by-step solutions:</b> Each step with the rule that was used, for equations, systems, inequalities, derivatives, integrals, limits, matrices, Taylor polynomials, differential equations and more</li>
<li><b>Equations and inequalities:</b> Single equations, linear and many non-linear systems, inequalities with interval notation and number line</li>
<li><b>Calculus:</b> Derivatives (also at a point and partial), antiderivatives and definite integrals (numeric when there is no formula), sums, products, limits (also one-sided), Taylor polynomials</li>
<li><b>Differential equations:</b> First-order linear and separable, and second-order linear equations with constant coefficients, with or without initial conditions</li>
<li><b>Matrices and vectors:</b> Determinant, inverse, transpose, row reduction (rref), eigenvalues and eigenvectors; dot and cross product, norm, angle, projection and unit vector</li>
<li><b>Complex numbers:</b> Rectangular and polar form (r∠θ), re, im, arg and conj</li>
<li><b>Scientific functions:</b> Trigonometric functions with their reciprocals, hyperbolic functions, all their inverses, logarithms to any base, factorials, Γ, n-th roots, modulo</li>
<li><b>Function picker:</b> Searchable list of further functions for statistics, combinatorics, probability distributions, rounding and number theory, random numbers and special functions</li>
<li><b>Variables:</b> x, y, z and any other lower-case letter; store values, matrices and vectors with := and reuse the last result with Ans</li>
<li><b>Units:</b> Calculate and convert across 18 categories (length, mass, energy, pressure, temperature, electricity, …) with metric, imperial or US customary result preferences</li>
<li><b>Constants:</b> Searchable library of mathematical and physical constants with their units</li>
<li><b>Graph plotter:</b> Functions, implicit curves, parametric lines and 3D surfaces; points snap to zeros, extrema, inflection points and intersections; trace, tangent and area tools; logarithmic axes and polar coordinates</li>
<li><b>Programmer mode:</b> Hexadecimal, octal and binary input with bitwise operations and shifts</li>
<li><b>History and sessions:</b> Reuse, copy or delete earlier calculations, with undo; save a session to a text file and open it again</li>
<li><b>In-app help:</b> Searchable guide to every key and function, with examples you can put straight into the line</li>
<li><b>Languages:</b> English, German, French, Spanish, Russian and Simplified Chinese</li>
<li><b>Display options:</b> Fixed or automatic decimal places, digit grouping, point or comma as decimal separator, light/dark/system theme, three-page keypad that is shown side by side on tablets and foldables</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>

No ads, no tracking, no network access. Everything is calculated on your device.
