# Privacy Policy

**Arithmeticus Calculator**
Effective date: October 22, 2026

Arithmeticus Calculator is a free and open-source app developed by PcmciaKai. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app. In short: **the app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.**

## Data collection

The app does not collect any personal or usage data. It contains no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking libraries. It does not request the internet permission and therefore cannot send any data off your device.

## Data stored on your device

To work properly, the app stores the following information locally on your device only:

- Your settings (for example language, theme, decimal separator and display options)
- Your calculation history, stored variables and the current input

This data never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time by clearing the history in the app, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app in your Android settings.

## Files

When you save or open a session, the app uses the Android system file picker. The app only writes to or reads from the file you select yourself, and has no access to any other files on your device.

## Clipboard

The app accesses the clipboard only when you choose to copy or paste a calculation or result.

## Backups

If you have enabled Android's device backup, Android may include the app's settings and history in your device backup. That backup is handled by Android and your backup provider (such as your Google account) under their privacy policies; the developer has no access to it.

## Permissions

The app does not request any runtime permissions.

## Children

The app does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this location with a new effective date. The full history of changes is visible in this project's source repository.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at <https://github.com/PcmciaKai/Arithmeticus-Calculator/issues>.
